Verdana has two weights

Regular and bold, nothing between. So the ramp uses 400 and 700 only — the old 500 and 600 steps are gone rather than left to the browser to fake. Rows stepped down a size too: Verdana sets wider, and a history row at the old size ran past the edge of a phone.

Numbers are always tabular

Positions, points per match, scorelines and player counts all set font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums, so a standings column stays a column and a score does not jump between 9 and 10.

Nothing on a phone below 13px

The 11px step is caps-only and short — a court name, a section label. Every sentence the organizer has to read outdoors sits at 13px or above, and every tap target at 44px or above. The readout above turns red when a scale breaks that floor.
