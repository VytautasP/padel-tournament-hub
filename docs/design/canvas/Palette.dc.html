Colour tokens

A proposal for the top half of styles.css. Nine existing tokens are re-valued; nine are new. Everything below is a raw palette value — components keep naming only the Tailwind utilities the @theme inline block maps them to. The brand is a lever: change it and every value derived from it, in both themes, moves with it.

Two roles, not a scale: a card resting on the page, and a sheet lifted over a scrim. The dark values are not the light ones dimmed — ink at 6% is invisible on a dark surface, so dark shadows go black and much stronger, and lean on surface-raised being lighter than surface to do half the separating.

Three metals, no panel

The celebration lives on the medals, not on the panel. The block itself is just the brand at a whisper — --palette-brand-wash, a token rather than an opacity, because the two themes do not agree on the strength: 5% carries on the light ramp and 10% is what the dark one needs — with a brand border — so gold, silver and bronze are the only warm colour on the screen, and the podium no longer needs a surface and line of its own. The medal tracks the position, so a joint first shows gold, gold, bronze — and no silver at all (decision #8). It sits beside the position, never replacing it: the number is the fact, the medal is the decoration, and only one of them needs reading aloud.

Warning, then danger

Two tones, two jobs. Warning marks a team that is short a player — something to fix, not something that went wrong. Danger is for the three sentences that cannot be undone: discard a session, delete a history row, end a session. Neither appears in a running round.
